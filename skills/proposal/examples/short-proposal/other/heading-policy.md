# Title Case Heading Policy

Use Title Case for non-core proposal headings, including optional or domain-specific H2s
and H3s. Preserve established technical terminology, identifiers, and other
capitalization that carries technical meaning. Capitalization must not change Markdown
heading levels, section order, or the meaning of established engineering terms.

This guidance does not authorize changing the proposal format's eight fixed core H2
labels. Preserve their exact wording, capitalization, and order:

- `Table of Contents`
- `Recommendation`
- `Technical Rationale`
- `Questions`
- `Options Considered`
- `Implementation Details`
- `Verification Criteria`
- `Sources`

Preserve the existing table-of-contents text for the seven content sections exactly;
do not include `Table of Contents` in its own table of contents.
