# Data Quality Analysis

## Define Fitness For Use

- Identify the decision, model, report, or workflow that consumes the data.
- Define required dimensions of quality, scope, timeliness, and acceptable error.
- Identify data sources, owners, transformations, and storage locations.
- Establish the population and time period represented by the data.

## Measure Quality

- Assess completeness, accuracy, consistency, validity, uniqueness, and timeliness.
- Trace lineage from source collection through transformations and outputs.
- Identify missing values, duplicates, outliers, schema drift, and contradictory records.
- Assess sampling bias, measurement bias, and coverage gaps against the intended use.

## Improve Reliability

- Rank defects by effect on the consuming decision or system.
- Define correction, exclusion, enrichment, or recollection actions for material defects.
- Add automated quality checks at the earliest controllable point in the lineage.
- Document remaining limitations and restrict unsupported uses.
