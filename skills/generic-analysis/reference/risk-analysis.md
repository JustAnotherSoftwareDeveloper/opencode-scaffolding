# Risk Analysis

## Identify Risks

- Define each risk as a condition, event, and resulting consequence.
- Identify sources of uncertainty across technical, operational, financial, legal, safety, privacy, and schedule domains.
- Separate risks from existing issues, assumptions, and constraints.
- Record triggers and early indicators for material risks.

## Assess Exposure

- Estimate likelihood using available evidence and relevant base rates.
- Estimate impact across affected stakeholders, assets, objectives, and time horizons.
- Assess detectability, reversibility, and correlation with other risks.
- Prioritize risks by exposure rather than likelihood alone.

## Plan Responses

- Select avoidance, reduction, transfer, acceptance, or contingency actions.
- Assign an owner, trigger, response action, and monitoring signal to each material risk.
- Evaluate the cost and effectiveness of mitigations.
- State residual risk after planned controls.
