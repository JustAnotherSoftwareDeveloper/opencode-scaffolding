# Artifact Analysis

## Inspect The Artifact

- Identify the artifact type, version, provenance, and intended purpose.
- Inspect structure, metadata, dependencies, and relevant surrounding context.
- Extract observable statements, behavior, values, and inconsistencies.
- Preserve exact locations for material findings.

## Evaluate Findings

- Compare observed content against requirements, specifications, or expected behavior.
- Trace material findings across related artifacts and execution paths.
- Separate confirmed defects from suspicious patterns and unsupported interpretations.
- Assess the impact, reachability, and reproducibility of each confirmed finding.

## Report Results

- Cite the artifact location and evidence for every material finding.
- Rank findings by severity, confidence, and affected scope.
- Identify missing artifacts or access limits that constrain the analysis.
- Propose verification steps before making irreversible changes.
