# Feasibility Analysis

## Define The Proposal

- State the proposed outcome, scope, required capabilities, and delivery horizon.
- Identify mandatory constraints and minimum viable acceptance criteria.
- Separate validated requirements from assumptions and preferences.
- Define the consequences of partial delivery or delay.

## Test Constraints

- Assess technical capability, integration complexity, reliability, and maintainability.
- Assess operational capacity, staffing, process maturity, and support requirements.
- Assess legal, regulatory, contractual, privacy, and policy constraints.
- Assess funding, cost, schedule, procurement, and opportunity-cost constraints.

## Determine Viability

- Identify blocking constraints and the actions required to remove them.
- Compare feasible delivery paths by scope, cost, time, and risk.
- Define a reversible validation step for uncertain assumptions.
- State whether the proposal is feasible, conditionally feasible, or infeasible within the stated constraints.
