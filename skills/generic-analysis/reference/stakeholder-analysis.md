# Stakeholder Analysis

## Identify Stakeholders

- Identify individuals, groups, systems, and institutions affected by the outcome.
- Record each stakeholder's role, authority, dependency, and exposure.
- Distinguish decision makers, implementers, beneficiaries, and risk bearers.
- Include stakeholders who lack formal authority but face material consequences.

## Assess Interests

- Identify each stakeholder's goals, incentives, constraints, and concerns.
- Map conflicts, shared interests, and information asymmetries.
- Assess influence over decisions, resources, approvals, and implementation.
- Validate assumptions about stakeholder interests with direct evidence when available.

## Shape Engagement

- Prioritize engagement by impact, influence, urgency, and decision relevance.
- Define information, consultation, approval, or collaboration needs for each priority group.
- Surface unresolved conflicts before selecting an option.
- Document ownership and communication actions required for execution.
