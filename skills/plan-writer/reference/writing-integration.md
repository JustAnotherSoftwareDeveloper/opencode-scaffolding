- After collecting documentation skills, reconcile the exact record named `markdown-writing-guide`, with `class: documentation` and the collector-winning `SKILL.md` path. - Treat the guide as passive context for plan-writer, not as an operation. Write task fields for the person who will execute or review the task.
- Apply it to compatible substantive prose authored or substantively revised in packet `summary` and task `purpose`, `context`, `executionInstructions`, `verification`, and `expectedOutput`. Preserve each field's existing schema and task-contract meaning, especially `summary` as a one-paragraph account of the request's goal, scope, and constraints and each `purpose` as a single actionable, self-contained sentence describing one task result. - Keep `tasks.md` as deterministic rendering of validated task JSON. Do not use the guide to revise the renderer's fixed layout or mechanically rendered values; substantive prose remains the responsibility of its packet/task-field author.

## Assigning The Guide To Generated Tasks

- During the existing post-boundary assignment, select the exact collector-winning `markdown-writing-guide` record as `class: documentation` when the task requires substantive Markdown or Markdown-like prose to be authored or revised and the guide adds compatible value. This includes prose within a specialized artifact when the guide complements, rather than replaces, that artifact's local writing guidance and contract. A specialized format or operation class alone is not a reason to omit it. Fixed field names and positions do not turn authored prose into rendering; the operation remains responsible.
- Do not select it for deterministic rendering of existing fixed-field data into Markdown, mechanical edits that author or revise no substantive prose, path-only `.md` work, or non-prose tasks. Decide from the prose the task asks to author or revise, not its extension.
- Reconcile the record's exact name, class, and path. Block an applicable assignment if the record is absent or mismatched; do not substitute another skill.
- The guide is never an operation owner and counts toward the existing maximum of two documentation assignments. Do not assign it automatically merely because plan-writer loaded it for its own authoring. Select it only when the generated task meets the prose condition above and it fits the existing assignment limit; keep exactly one operation owner and no more than two materially relevant documentation skills.

## Bounded Correction

- Before a substantive prose correction, load the guide in a direct workflow and read its writing-quality reference. In packet execution, use it only when declared and already loaded, then read that reference.
- Keep corrections within the exact finding-authorized fields. The guide does not grant broader edit authority.
